Good to know

Can’t find it here? Email admin@screendemons.com.

Does Screen Demons see which apps I use?

No. Only your daily screen-time total is shared, with friends you challenge and circles you join. Which apps you use never leaves your phone. The apps you choose for mindful pauses stay on your device.

Is it free?

It’s free to download. An optional Premium subscription is on the way, and the details will be in the app before anything is charged.

Is it on Android?

iPhone is available now. Android is coming soon: join the waitlist below and we’ll email you the day it’s live.

Why does it need Screen Time access?

Your daily total is what powers your demon’s mood, your streak and challenges with friends. Without it there’s nothing to measure, so it’s the one permission the app requires. Notifications and mindful pauses are optional.

How do mindful pauses work?

You pick the apps you open without thinking. When you open one, Screen Demons shows a short guided reset, then you choose to close the app or continue. On Android this uses Accessibility, only to notice which of your chosen apps is in front. It never reads what’s on your screen.

What if a friend doesn’t have the app?

Challenges are between people in the app, so send them your invite link and they can join in a minute.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time from Profile. Your account and data are permanently deleted 24 hours later, and you can restore it in that time if you change your mind. See how deletion works.
