Delete your Screen Demons account

You can delete your account and its data in the app, or by asking us.

In the app

1. Open Screen Demons and go to Profile. 2. Scroll down and tap Delete Account, then confirm. 3. Your account is scheduled for deletion and is permanently deleted 24 hours later. If you sign back in during those 24 hours you can restore it.

Without the app

Email admin@screendemons.com from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your profile and photo, friends and friend requests, circles memberships, challenges you took part in (challenges you created are kept for the other person, without your name), goals, screen-time logs, notifications and analytics data. A record that an account was deleted is kept in a form that cannot identify you.

What is not deleted

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
